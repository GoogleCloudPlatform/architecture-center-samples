Returns one person's HR record as a flat list for public-records review: identity (name and the national ID masked to its last four digits), disciplinary actions, grievances, the latest 12 paychecks (gross and net only), absence history and attachments (title and file name, no content). Each row has a record type, key, date, title, a detail text and up to two amounts. EMPLID is required; at most 300 rows; rows of different types are listed one after another instead of being joined, so nothing is multiplied.
Use it to assemble what exists for one person before redaction. The full national ID, bank details, address and long-text descriptions are not returned. The FSCM-based tool of the same purpose (which returned the unmasked national ID) is disabled. There is no FOIA skill in google_oracle_skills yet; this toolset is a placeholder until one is delivered.
Underlying Tables: PS_NAMES, PS_PERS_NID, PS_DISCIPLIN_ACTN, PS_GRIEVANCE, PS_PAY_CHECK, PS_ABSENCE_HIST, PS_GP_ABS_ATTACH, PS_HR_PER_BIOG_ATT, PS_BEN_ATTACH_DTL.
Security Context: Highly sensitive personal data; use the PL/SQL wrapper and an authorised user in production.
