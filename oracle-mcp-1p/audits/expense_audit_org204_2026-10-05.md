Observations (leads for follow-up, not findings of fraud):
- Real, separate business trips are unlikely to produce a dinner, hotel stay and car rental that match to the cent every month. Possible explanations are a copied template or recurring charges. Each needs documentary support, and with no receipts the duplicate-billing test (2 CFR 200.403(c), 200.405) cannot be completed.
- Sightseeing car rentals ('Ferrari', 'BMW-cabriolet', 'Rolls Royce') appear on 31 lines totaling $19,714.51 across several employees, with identical wording. Concert and baseball tickets plus transport to the concert add $14,196.32. All are unallowable under 200.438.
- Free-text wording repeats across unrelated employees ('Dinner with Client's Project Management', 'Only Executive Suite available', 'Monthly team call'). This suggests copying, or seeded test data if this is a demonstration database. If it is test data, the control findings (no receipts, no policy flags, Entertainment mapped to a Miscellaneous rule) still stand as a configuration review.
- Hotel lines that state nights imply $136.00 to $252.50 per night. No locality is recorded, so none can be tested against GSA. Hotel 'Meals (3 days)' lines and meals coded as Hotel or Entertainment indicate expense types are being used inconsistently (2 CFR 200.403(c)).
- Receipts are inconsistent across the three places EBS records them. There is no electronic image on any line; 48 of 50 report headers still say paper receipts are REQUIRED and never received; but 301 of 305 lines are flagged verified. All 50 reports were nevertheless paid in full (1 to 11 days after submission), and the policy engine raised no violation.
- Airfare: 45 of 47 airfares ($103,751.88) are domestic business class; only 2 are coach. 2 CFR 200.475(e) allows only the coach fare unless an exception is documented.
- Meals record an attendee count on 51 lines; per-attendee amounts are shown in the matrices. Example: W39254's $981 dinner lists 9 attendees ($109.00 each). Attendee first names are recorded on the line, which is not enough to tell employees from clients.
- Not one of the 305 lines carries an award, project or task number, so nothing in this sample is charged to a grant. The funding stream (Principle 1) therefore cannot be shown to be federal; the 2 CFR 200 baseline is applied as the skill directs, but it may not be the governing regime for these costs. `ebs_get_award_terms` lists 50 awards in the instance (all under a different operating unit, 1448); none of the 305 sampled lines references one.
- A duplicate scan with `ebs_find_duplicate_expense_lines` over org 204 reports submitted 2010-07-01 to 2010-10-31 (lines of $100 or more) found 56 groups where one employee repeats the same line (type, amount, wording) on two or more reports, 56 of them on three reports, and no same-day double entries. Some groups include reports outside the 50 sampled, for example W38944 (employee 972's third Ferrari rental), which shows the recurrence extends beyond this sample.
- No expense date falls after its report's submission date, and every report reconciles: line amounts sum to the report total.
